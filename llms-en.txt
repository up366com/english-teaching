# 天学网 · 英语教学

> 北京天学网教育科技股份有限公司 — AI 驱动英语教与学 · 让每一节课都有效

Tianxuewang (UP366) is a Beijing-based education-technology company building an AI
English-teaching system around its in-house Tianxue large language model. Its product line
covers speaking assessment and practice, AI writing and dictation marking, listening and
vocabulary resources, and a teaching platform used by teachers for lesson preparation,
homework and class-level learning analytics.
